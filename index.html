Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the 405 Facebook Page bot ("we", "our", "the bot") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with our Page on Facebook Messenger.

1. What We Collect

When you send a message to the 405 Page, our bot may receive:

  • Message content — the text you send to the bot
  • Page-Scoped ID (PSID) — a unique identifier Facebook assigns to you for this specific Page
  • Timestamp — when the message was sent

We do not collect your real Facebook user ID, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal data.

2. How We Use It

Your message content and PSID are used solely to:

  • Generate an automated response to your message
  • Maintain conversation context (so the bot can remember what was said earlier in the same chat)
  • Apply rate limits so one user cannot spam the bot

3. Third-Party Services

To generate replies, our bot sends the text of your message to Groq, an AI inference provider. Groq's handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at https://groq.com/privacy-policy/.

Messages are also transmitted through Meta's Messenger Platform, subject to Meta's privacy policies.

4. Data Retention

Conversation history is stored in memory only on our server. It is automatically cleared when:

  • The bot restarts, or
  • You send the command /ai clear

We do not write message logs to disk, and we do not store any data in a database.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, except as required to operate the bot (i.e., the Groq API and Meta's Messenger Platform, as described above).

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Stop using the bot at any time by simply not messaging the Page
  • Clear your conversation history by sending /ai clear
  • Request deletion of any data by contacting the Page admin

7. Children's Privacy

This bot is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
